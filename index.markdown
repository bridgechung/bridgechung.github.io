# Services

## Individual Coaching
 
Two-heads-one-hand is very popular format for 1 on 1 sessions. You and I share the same hand, and we discuss any and all decisions together. Really get inside my head and find out what I'm thinking in this very exact moment!  
![Lesson](/images/kuhchung-video.png){: width="291px" height="223px"} 

Often my students will ask me about rules or lessons they learned from elsewhere that don't quite make sense, and I explain the idea in very clear and simple terms. Gain understanding and reduce the stress of learning catchy phrases that don't actually teach you something!

## Partnership Coaching
Sharpen your partnership bidding and defense with me as an impartial third party to guide. Reduce partnership friction and frustration, gain empathy into the problems that your partner faces that you may have missed, and vice versa. A happy partnership is a healthy partnership!

## Group Lessons/Supervised Play
A group of 4 (or 3 + me) get together and play! I will discuss every hand, answer any and all questions that anyone has about any of their decisions, and everyone has a good time! I often travel to student locations for these sessions.

## Professional Play
Hire me to play bridge with you as your partner or as a teammate. Includes a post session hand review!
